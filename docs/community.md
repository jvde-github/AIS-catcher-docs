# Community

AIS-catcher is free and open source, and gets better with the help of the people who run it.

- **Put your station on the map.** Share your reception with the community map on [aiscatcher.org](https://aiscatcher.org): your station gets a name, coverage statistics and its own page. In managed mode, see [Outputs](managed/output.md); in manual mode, see [Community Feed](configuration/output/community-feed.md).
- **Ask and discuss.** Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/jvde-github/AIS-catcher/discussions) for questions and ideas, and [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jvde-github/AIS-catcher/issues) for bugs.
- **Share recordings.** A recording from a place where AIS-catcher struggles helps improve its decoding. Mention it in a discussion.
